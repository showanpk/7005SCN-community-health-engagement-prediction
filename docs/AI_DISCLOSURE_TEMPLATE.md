# AI Use Disclosure — Working Template

**Important:** This is a working disclosure template. It must be checked against the current Coventry University assessment guidance and edited so that it accurately reflects the student's actual use before submission.

AI-assisted work during the project included support with:

- understanding data-science concepts and model-evaluation metrics;
- discussing data-quality issues and target leakage;
- troubleshooting SQL/Python errors;
- reviewing the structure of analytical code;
- language, grammar and document organisation.

All SQL/Python code included in the artefact was executed by the student against the research environment, outputs were reviewed by the student, and methodological decisions should be explained and defended by the student in the report.

Because the assessment guidance controls what forms of AI assistance are permitted, the final disclosure should not omit or misrepresent any use. Where there is uncertainty about whether code assistance is permitted under the module's Amber category, the student should obtain clarification from the supervisor/module leader before final submission.

Record the specific AI tool, dates of access, representative prompts, outputs used, and how the outputs were validated or changed, as required by the module brief.
